# Grade Security+ Quiz and Record Progress

Grade my submitted answers against the answer key for the
current quiz. Preserve the question IDs and original source
references.

For each question:
1. Mark the answer correct, incorrect, or partially correct
   where the question format supports partial credit.
2. Explain the correct answer and why plausible alternatives
   are wrong.
3. Cite the exact bootcamp PDF/part/page and relevant notes file.
4. Identify the underlying concept being tested.
5. Distinguish a knowledge gap from a correct guess when I
   supplied confidence information.
6. Flag any answer-key or source discrepancy rather than
   silently grading against questionable material.

At the end:
- Calculate the score and report performance by objective.
- List missed concepts and concepts answered correctly by guessing.
- Identify patterns in mistakes.
- Recommend the most valuable remediation actions.

Update user_progress/ with the date, question IDs, objective IDs,
results, weak concepts, and review recommendations.

Do not overwrite earlier history. Append a new session record
or update the existing progress format consistently.

Do not mark an objective mastered from a single small quiz.
