# Security+ Spaced-Repetition Session

Read user_progress/ and the historical quiz records.

Identify concepts due for review using previous performance,
confidence, and time since the last successful recall.

Prioritize:
1. Repeatedly missed concepts.
2. Previously guessed concepts.
3. Concepts due for delayed review.
4. Older concepts that have not appeared in recent quizzes.

Generate a manageable review quiz using fresh questions.
Use source-grounded notes and preserve question IDs and citations
in the answer key.

Do not reveal answers before submission.

After grading:
- Update the review history and last-tested dates.
- Record consecutive successful and unsuccessful recalls.
- Schedule the next review using increasing intervals when recall
  is successful and shorter intervals when recall fails.
- Avoid marking concepts mastered after one successful attempt.

Use an explicit, consistent review schedule, such as approximately
1, 3, 7, 14, and 30 days after learning, adjusted for performance.
Do not claim to have scheduled notifications or background tasks.
Record due dates so they can be checked at the next study session.
