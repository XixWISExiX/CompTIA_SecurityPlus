# CompTIA Security+ SY0-701 Exam Readiness Assessment

Read objective_mapping.md, coverage_tracker.md, user_progress/,
and the validated course notes.

Assess readiness across all 28 official exam sub-objectives.

Requirements:
1. Distinguish unextracted, unverified, unpracticed, and weak
   objectives. Missing evidence is not proof of mastery.
2. Summarize practice performance by objective using available
   historical results.
3. Identify the highest-priority remaining gaps.
4. Generate a mixed, exam-style quiz weighted toward weak areas,
   while retaining some coverage of previously learned concepts.
5. Include scenario-based questions and plausible distractors.
6. Validate answer keys and source references before presenting.
7. Do not reveal answers until I submit my responses.
8. Grade the quiz, record results, and revise the readiness
   assessment based on evidence.

Do not claim I am exam-ready based solely on notes completion
or a single high-scoring quiz.

Report coverage gaps separately from demonstrated knowledge gaps.
Recommend the next study actions based on the evidence available.
